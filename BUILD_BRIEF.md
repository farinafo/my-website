# Portfolio Site Brief

## Goal and Positioning

Present Fan Chen as a research- and strategy-oriented professional who combines data analysis, market intelligence, product thinking, and international experience.

Core capability pillars:

- Data & Research: analytics, machine learning, econometrics, market and industry research, user research, and competitive intelligence.
- Strategy & Business: product and market strategy, global GTM, business analysis, investment analysis, entrepreneurship, and commercialization.
- International & Policy: United Nations experience, international development interests, cross-cultural project management, global markets, and policy environments.

Areas of focus: AI & Digital Economy, Global Markets, and Real Assets & Infrastructure. International Development is an emerging direction and should be supported by real experience and research rather than an empty project category.

Suggested homepage headline: “Research, Strategy & Data across AI, Global Markets & Real Assets.”

Suggested supporting statement: “I combine quantitative analysis, market research and product thinking to turn complex information into strategic decisions across technology, international markets and real assets.”

Do not present Social Media, Content Creator, Marketing Generalist, Product Operations, UI/UX, or Architecture Portfolio Candidate as the central career identity. They may appear as accurate parts of experience.

## Audience and Content Principles

The primary audience is recruiters, hiring managers, and interviewers. The site should help them understand the professional direction, evidence, role, and limits of each project quickly.

- Distinguish personal work from team work.
- Distinguish analysis, recommendation, proposal, modeled result, and implemented outcome.
- Use metrics only with clear definitions, time periods, comparison conditions, and evidence.
- Do not invent adoption, revenue, growth, investment, or policy outcomes.
- Do not describe academic models as production systems without validation evidence.
- Keep Chinese and English content accurate and aligned.

## Existing Visual System and Technology

Preserve the site's restrained editorial system: existing serif, sans-serif, and mono typography; paper-like surfaces; neutral palette; fine borders; generous spacing; restrained motion; responsive layouts; and existing project-cover treatment.

Keep the current Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion stack. Keep the current routes and project detail URLs. Do not add a CMS, database, new framework, large animation system, or a new design system. Do not use gradients or glass effects as a new visual direction.

The homepage must expose its professional content in normal vertical reading order. Draggable windows and auto-scrolling visual experiments may remain as secondary decoration, but must not carry essential career information.

## Case Study and Research Standards

Keep the existing reusable project detail template. A case should make the question, role, evidence, method, decision, outcome, and limitations clear. Add source links and adoption evidence when available; do not rewrite every project into a generic template.

- Hedonic Regression: coefficient −0.0703 in a double-log model means about a 0.0703% price association for a 1% distance change. With p ≈ 0.085, it is marginal at 10%, not significant at 5%, and not causal evidence.
- Shanghai Housing ML: retain the 2000–2024 scope, macroeconomic / financial / policy variables, XGBoost, SHAP, RMSE / MAE, and limitations. Do not claim a 98% improvement without a clear comparable benchmark. State interpolation and validation constraints; do not call it production-ready.
- Monza: label revenue, rent, ROI, and NPV as projected, modeled, estimated, or scenario-based.
- Casa Rossi and Zhaohua Temple: distinguish analysis, recommendation, and proposal from implemented outcomes. Identify team contributions where relevant.

Notes may stand on their research content and need not end with a product-management lesson. Lab is for creative experiments and AI visual exploration, not the primary career signal.
